About Prakash Hospital

Prakash Hospital – Excellence in Care

Prakash Hospital, established in 2005, is a leading orthopedic and diagnostic care center in Faridabad. We are dedicated to providing trusted, quality healthcare through advanced medical technology, experienced specialists, and compassionate patient care.

Our hospital has performed more than 2 lakh ultrasound scans, treated over 1.5 lakh patients, and completed 15,000+ orthopedic procedures. We offer 24/7 orthopedic emergency support and a full range of radiology and pathology services to ensure accurate diagnosis and effective treatment.

Our goal is to provide ethical, affordable, and evidence-based healthcare that prioritizes patient well-being and long-term recovery.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common queries about our services and care.

We provide comprehensive orthopedic services including joint replacement (knee, hip), trauma care, fracture management, arthroscopy, sports injury treatment, spine care, and post-surgical physiotherapy. Our team uses advanced techniques for faster recovery and better outcomes.

You can book an appointment online through our website using the appointment form below, or call us directly at +91-9876543210. We also offer WhatsApp booking for your convenience.

Yes, we accept all major health insurance plans. Our billing team will assist you with the insurance paperwork and ensure a smooth cashless treatment process.

Yes, we have a 24/7 emergency department equipped with a trauma team, ambulance services and advanced life support systems. You can call our emergency number +91-9876543210 for immediate assistance.

The cost of knee replacement varies depending on the type of implant, hospital stay and associated medical services. We provide transparent pricing and a detailed estimate before the procedure. Please contact us for a personalized quote.

Fracture recovery depends on the type and severity of the fracture. Simple fractures may heal in 6-8 weeks, while complex fractures may take 3-6 months. Our orthopedic team will provide a tailored recovery plan with physiotherapy for optimal healing.

Yes, we have an in-house physiotherapy and rehabilitation department. Our expert physiotherapists provide post-surgery rehab, exercise therapy, pain management and sports injury rehabilitation services.

You can contact our doctors by booking an appointment through our website or by calling our reception. For urgent queries, you can also reach out via our WhatsApp number or email at info@prakashhospital.com.
